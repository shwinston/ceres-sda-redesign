No. 03 · First Visit

What to expect

No pressure, no spotlight. Here’s how a Saturday morning goes.

  1. 1
    Arrive a few minutes earlyA greeter can help you find a seat or a class confirm greeters.
  2. 2
    9:30 a.m. · Sabbath SchoolRelaxed Bible study in small classes, with classes for kids.
  3. 3
    11:00 a.m. · WorshipSinging, prayer, Scripture and a Bible-based message.

Wear whatever you’re comfortable in confirm wording. Many Christians worship on Sunday; Adventists keep the seventh-day Sabbath, Saturday.
