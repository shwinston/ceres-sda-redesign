Ways to Give

Thank you for your faithful generosity.

Header photo: Almond harvest windrows, California. USDA NRCS photo, public domain.

Give online

Return tithe and give offerings securely through AdventistGiving, the official giving platform of the Adventist Church in North America. Give once or set up recurring gifts.

Give with AdventistGiving

By mail

Make checks payable to Ceres Seventh-day Adventist Church, confirm payee and mail to:
1633 Central Ave
Ceres, CA 95307

Please don't send cash by mail.

In person

Use a tithe envelope during the offering at worship. Envelopes are in the pew racks confirm.

Where your gift goes

Tithe supports pastoral ministry across our conference. Local church budget keeps our doors open and ministries running. Community Services stocks the food bank and clothing closet. Add special projects or building funds and their goals

QR code placeholder
Add a QR code linking to our AdventistGiving page for the bulletin and screens.

Questions about giving? Contact the church treasurer through the contact form.
