What We Believe

The heart of our faith in plain words.

Header photo: “Open Bible with pen” by Ryk Neethling, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

One God

We believe in one God: Father, Son and Holy Spirit, a unity of three persons who love us and work together for our salvation.

Jesus saves

Jesus lived a sinless life, died in our place, and rose again on the third day. Salvation is a free gift received by faith, not earned.

The Bible speaks

Scripture is God's voice speaking personally to us today, a road map for life and the foundation of everything we believe.

A day of rest

The seventh-day Sabbath (Friday sunset to Saturday sunset) is God's weekly gift of rest, worship and connection.

Jesus is coming again

Jesus promised to return. That hope shapes how we live, love and serve while we wait.

Whole-person living

God cares about all of life: how we eat, think, rest, treat others and care for creation. That's why Adventists value health and service.
