Why do we worship on Saturday?

In the very first pages of the Bible, God rested on the seventh day and blessed it (Genesis 2:2–3). Later, the fourth commandment invites us to “remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy” (Exodus 20:8–11). Jesus himself went to the synagogue on the Sabbath “as was His custom” (Luke 4:16).

For Adventists, the Sabbath (from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset) is a weekly gift: a 24-hour break from work and screens to rest, worship, spend time with family and serve others. It's not about earning God's love; it's about enjoying it.

Common questions

Do I have to be an Adventist to attend?

Not at all. Everyone is welcome, whatever your background or questions.

Will I be singled out as a visitor?

No. You may be warmly greeted, but you won't be asked to stand up or speak. confirm

Is there an offering?

An offering is collected during worship. Guests are never expected to give.

Is there a pastor?

We're currently seeking a new pastor. Our elders lead worship with guest speakers, and our church family is glad to welcome you.
