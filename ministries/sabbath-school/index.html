Sabbath School

Saturdays at 9:30 a.m.: small groups, open Bibles, real conversation.

Header photo: “Open Bible with pen” by Ryk Neethling, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

Bible study for every age

Every Saturday at 9:30 a.m., before worship, we gather in small classes to study the Bible together. Adult classes follow the worldwide Sabbath School quarterly and are discussion-based. Ask questions, share, or simply listen.

Children's classes

Kids learn Bible stories through songs, crafts and activities in age-group classes. list classes, e.g. Beginner, Kindergarten, Primary, Junior, Earliteen; rooms & leaders
